    Working tips

      Ratios are estimates from a paint-mixing model (Kubelka–Munk) using typical pigment data. Brands vary in tinting strength, so mix the first tone, dab it on a card next to the matching patch of your printed reference, and adjust. Parts are by volume, measured with the palette knife.
